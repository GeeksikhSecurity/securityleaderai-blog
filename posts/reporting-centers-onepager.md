# Where to Report Cyber Fraud — Global Directory

If you have been targeted by cyber fraud, report it to your government's official agency below. Reporting is free, takes only minutes, and helps protect others. **Always report to your local police as well**, especially if you have lost money.

This directory covers five regions where the Sikh diaspora and broader Panjabi-speaking community lives in significant numbers. Phone numbers, URLs, and agency scope were verified against each agency's own `.gov` / national-police domain on **2026-05-07** (helplines re-verified **2026-06-18**).

---

## Free helplines — call first if you are not sure

If you are not sure whether something is a scam, or you have just been targeted, you can phone a free helpline and a real person will talk it through with you. You do not have to be a member, and you do not have to decide alone.

| Country   | Free helpline                                   | Number            |
| --------- | ----------------------------------------------- | ----------------- |
| USA       | AARP Fraud Watch Network                        | **877-908-3360**  |
| Canada    | Canadian Anti-Fraud Centre (CAFC)               | **1-888-495-8501** |
| UK        | Citizens Advice consumer helpline               | **0808 223 1133** |
| Australia / NZ | IDCARE                                     | **1800 595 160**  |
| India     | National Cyber Crime Helpline                   | **1930**          |

These helplines give advice and support. To file a formal report, use the country sections below.

---

## What to do first

1. **If you have lost money** — call your bank immediately to stop or reverse transactions. Banks can often hold funds in transit for 24–72 hours.
2. **Save evidence** — take screenshots of messages, emails, websites, and transaction details *before deleting anything*. Date and time stamps matter.
3. **Phone a free helpline** (above) if you want to talk to someone before you act.
4. **Report to the agency below** for your country.
5. **Report to your local police** as well, especially if money is involved. The local police can act on local jurisdiction; the federal agency tracks the pattern.
6. **Tell your family** — particularly elders and anyone else who may receive similar messages. Most cyber fraud succeeds because the person targeted does not know to ask.

## United States

| Agency | What it handles | Website / Phone |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **AARP Fraud Watch Network** | Free helpline — ask if something is a scam, or get help after being targeted | **877-908-3360** |
| **IC3 (FBI Internet Crime Complaint Center)** | All internet-enabled crimes, online fraud, scams, identity theft, ransomware | [ic3.gov](https://www.ic3.gov) |
| **FTC** | Consumer fraud and scam reporting | [reportfraud.ftc.gov](https://reportfraud.ftc.gov) |
| **FBI Tips** | Threats to life, terrorism, national security, ongoing crimes | [tips.fbi.gov](https://tips.fbi.gov) |

For most cyber fraud incidents — financial scams, romance fraud, identity theft, ransomware — IC3 is the right destination. The AARP helpline is the place to phone first if you simply want to check whether something is a scam. Use FBI Tips only when life is at risk or a federal crime is in progress.

## Canada

| Agency | What it handles | Website / Phone |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Canadian Anti-Fraud Centre (CAFC)** | National fraud reporting, intelligence, prevention — and a phone helpline | [antifraudcentre-centreantifraude.ca](https://antifraudcentre-centreantifraude.ca) • **1-888-495-8501** |
| **Report Cybercrime and Fraud (RCMP NC3 + CAFC)** | National reporting system for fraud and cybercrime; launched Nov 2025 | [reportcyberandfraud.canada.ca](https://reportcyberandfraud.canada.ca) |
| **RCMP NC3 (National Cybercrime Coordination Centre)** | Federal cybercrime coordination layer | [rcmp.ca/cybercrime](https://rcmp.ca/en/federal-policing/cybercrime/national-cybercrime-coordination-centre) |

The CAFC number doubles as both the ask-first helpline and the national reporting line. The Government of Canada consolidated cybercrime + fraud reporting at `reportcyberandfraud.canada.ca` in November 2025. The legacy URL `reportcybercrime.ca` still redirects but should not be linked as canonical — use the new canada.ca URL.

## United Kingdom

| Agency | What it handles | Website / Phone |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Citizens Advice consumer helpline** | Free advice — ask if something is a scam, or get help | **0808 223 1133** |
| **Report Fraud** (England, Wales, N. Ireland) | If you have lost money or been hacked | [reportfraud.police.uk](https://www.reportfraud.police.uk) • **0300 123 2040** |
| **NCSC SERS** (email phishing) | Forward suspicious emails to this address | report@phishing.gov.uk |
| **NCSC SMS reporting** | Forward suspicious text messages | Forward the SMS to **7726** |
| **Police Scotland** (Scotland) | If you have lost money or been hacked. Report Fraud does not cover Scotland. | **101** |

In the UK, advice and reporting are separate: phone **Citizens Advice (0808 223 1133)** to ask whether something is a scam, and **Report Fraud (0300 123 2040)** to file a report. **Important name change (December 2025):** the service formerly known as **Action Fraud** is now **Report Fraud**, at `reportfraud.police.uk`. The phone number is unchanged. Any older guidance referencing `actionfraud.police.uk` should be updated.

## Australia & New Zealand

| Agency | What it handles | Website / Phone |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **IDCARE** | Free support helpline (Australia & New Zealand) — get help after being targeted | **1800 595 160** |
| **Scamwatch** (National Anti-Scam Centre, ACCC) | Reporting scams and community alerts | [scamwatch.gov.au](https://www.scamwatch.gov.au) |
| **ReportCyber** (ASD ACSC) | Cybercrime, security incidents, online abuse | [cyber.gov.au/report](https://www.cyber.gov.au/report-and-recover/report) |
| **Australian Cyber Security Hotline** | 24/7 live support | **1300 CYBER1** (1300 292 371) |

IDCARE is the free helpline to phone first — it supports both Australia and New Zealand. Use Scamwatch to report scams, and ReportCyber for active cybercrime (hacked account, ransomware, business email compromise).

## India

| Agency | What it handles | Website / Phone |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **National Cyber Crime Helpline** | 24/7 financial fraud helpline — phone first to freeze fraudulent transactions | **1930** |
| **National Cyber Crime Reporting Portal (I4C, MHA)** | All cybercrimes; special focus on women & children and financial fraud | [cybercrime.gov.in](https://www.cybercrime.gov.in) |

The **1930** helpline is specifically optimized for *financial* cyber fraud — banks and the National Cybercrime Coordination Centre can coordinate to freeze attacker accounts within hours if reported quickly. Phone 1930 first if money has just gone; for non-financial cybercrime, use the portal directly.
